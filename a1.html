Grid-Scale Battery Storage: Cost Trends 2026

Meridian Energy Review — independent analysis, published 2026-09-14.

Turnkey capex for 4-hour lithium-iron-phosphate systems settled at roughly USD 248 per kWh in Q2 2026, a 19% decline year over year, driven by cell overcapacity in China and a shift to 314 Ah form factors.

Levelised cost of storage for daily-cycling utility projects ranges from USD 92 to 131 per MWh depending on augmentations schedule, financing cost and the number of cycles contractually committed.

Methodology note

Figures are drawn from twelve disclosed EPC contracts and three developer interviews. No vendor pricing was accepted without a public filing or a signed confirmation.
